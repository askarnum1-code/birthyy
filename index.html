A letter for you

Dear Salma,

Happy birthday, my favorite person. I wanted to write you something bigger than a text, because you deserve more than a quick “hbd” and a heart.

Thank you for being the friend who shows up. You remember the little things, you notice when I’m quiet, and you somehow know exactly when I need a joke and when I need silence. Not everyone gets a friend like that. I got you.

You make ordinary days feel like adventures. A walk, a snack, a boring ride, a trip to the shops. Somehow it all turns into a memory because you’re there laughing at me, or with me, or just making that face you make.

You are kind without making a show of it, strong even when you say you’re not, and funnier than you give yourself credit for. You make people around you feel safe and seen. I’ve watched you do it again and again, and it never stops amazing me.

I’m sorry for the times I’ve been annoying, late, or too busy. Thank you for forgiving me every time, and for never keeping score. I hope I show up for you the way you show up for me.

This year, I wish for you peace in your heart, success in everything you work for, health for you and the people you love, and so many reasons to smile that you lose count. You deserve a life as beautiful as you make mine.

Whatever comes next, good days or hard ones, I’m in your corner. Always. Never doubt that.

Happy birthday, Salma. I love you, and I’m so proud to call you my best friend. 💙

Forever yours,
your best friend
